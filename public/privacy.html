CollegeGo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

CollegeGo (package com.vaibhav.iiitsuratapp) is a campus app and faculty web portal built by Vaibhav Raj. It is an independent project and is not an official app of any college. It is currently used by students and faculty of partner colleges. This policy explains what information CollegeGo collects, why, who can see it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, institute email address, enrollment number, branch, section, admission year and role (student or faculty). You sign in with your institute email.
  • Profile photo: only if you choose to add one.
  • Academic records shown in the app: timetable, attendance, marks and grades, exam and quiz schedules, and leave requests. This data is entered by faculty or by you, and is shown to you and to the faculty who need it.
  • Content you create: complaints, academic doubts, counselling requests, meeting confirmations and feedback you send.
  • Location, only when you send an SOS alert: your approximate location at that moment, so the doctor and wardens can reach you. CollegeGo does not track your location in the background.
  • Camera and photos: only when you choose to attach a photo, for example to a complaint or a medical leave document.
  • Calendar: only if you choose to add an event to your phone calendar. CollegeGo does not read your calendar.
  • Device information and notification token: a push notification token, device type and app version, used to deliver notifications and fix problems.

2. How we use it

  • To show you your timetable, attendance, marks, notices and other campus information.
  • To let faculty and the institute respond to leave requests, complaints, doubts, SOS alerts and meetings.
  • To send notifications you need, such as announcements, class reminders, results, meeting invites and SOS alerts.
  • To keep the app secure and working.

We do not sell your data and we do not show advertising.

3. Who can see your information

  • Faculty and administrators of the institute can see student details and records relevant to their role, such as attendance, marks, leave requests and complaints addressed to them or to the complaints committee.
  • The Director's office can see complaints and their progress, SOS alerts, and can invite people to meetings.
  • The doctor and wardens see your name, phone details you provide and location when you send an SOS alert.

4. Service providers

CollegeGo runs on Google Firebase (authentication, database, file storage, cloud functions and hosting) and sends push notifications through the Expo notification service and Google/Apple push systems. These providers process data on our behalf to run the app. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections.

5. Retention and deletion

  • You can delete your account in the app: Profile > Privacy & account > Delete account. This deletes your profile, complaints and counselling requests.
  • Records the institute needs to keep, such as attendance, grades and leave records, stay with the institute after account deletion.
  • Some items are removed automatically after a short time, for example resolved doubts.
  • If you cannot use the app, see how to request deletion.

6. Your choices

  • You can deny or withdraw camera, photo, location and notification permissions in your phone settings. Features that need them will not work, but the rest of the app will.
  • You can ask us to correct or delete your data at any time using the contact below.

7. Children

CollegeGo is intended for college students and faculty. It is not directed at children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated and, for important changes, the app will tell you.
