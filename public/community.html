CollegeGo Community Guidelines

Last updated: 12 November 2026

CollegeGo lets club leads and faculty post events and notices that other students read. By signing in to CollegeGo you agree to these guidelines and to the Privacy Policy.

Zero tolerance. Objectionable content and abusive users are not tolerated. Posts that break these guidelines are removed, and accounts that post them can be suspended or removed from the app.

1. Not allowed

  • Harassment, bullying, threats or intimidation of any person.
  • Hate speech or content that attacks people for their religion, caste, gender, sexuality, disability, region or any other identity.
  • Sexual, explicit or violent content.
  • Spam, scams, misleading posts or impersonating another person, club or office.
  • Sharing someone's personal information (phone number, address, photos) without their permission.
  • Anything illegal, or anything that breaks your college's code of conduct.

2. Reporting a post

Tap ⋯ on any club event or notice and choose Report post, then pick a reason. Reports go to the CollegeGo admins, who review every report within 24 hours. If a post breaks these guidelines, it is removed and the author's posting access is taken away.

3. Blocking someone

Tap ⋯ on a post and choose Block. You will no longer see events or notices from that person. Blocked people are listed under Profile → Blocked people, where you can unblock them at any time.

4. Contact

To report something urgent, or to appeal a decision, email collegego05@gmail.com.
