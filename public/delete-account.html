Delete your CollegeGo account

CollegeGo (com.vaibhav.iiitsuratapp)

In the app

Open Profile > Privacy & account > Delete account and confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

By email

If you cannot open the app, email collegego05@gmail.com from your institute email address with the subject "Delete my CollegeGo account". We will delete your account within 7 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, including name, photo and contact details.
  • Your Lost & Found posts, complaints, doubts and counselling requests.
  • Your notification token and device information.

What stays

Academic records the institute keeps, such as attendance, grades and leave records, stay with the institute and are not removed by deleting the app account.
